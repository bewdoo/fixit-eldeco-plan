Eldeco · 7 Peaks Residences · Omicron 1A, Greater Noida

Go-to-market plan for the promoter

The opportunity

7 Peaks sells a 2030 home at close to today's ready-flat price, with the airport already flying.

At ₹13,000 a sq ft it is within 4% of what ATS Dolce's ready flats resell for, and 13% below the median of the branded launches around it. Godrej Arden, 4 km away, booked ₹1,529 Cr in its launch quarter at about the same rate. The demand is there. The job is to tell 7 Peaks' story in one voice.

The risk

Brokers are telling the story, and getting it wrong. Live ads call 7 Peaks "Greater Noida (West)" and "pre-launch" nine months after launch. A look-alike site, eldecongroup.com, promises keys in mid-2028; RERA says November 2030. One broker's site ran 11 Google ads in 30 days, more than Eldeco's own 9.

But the market has slowed. Noida and Greater Noida sales fell 20% year on year in Apr–Jun 2026, and launches fell 72%. Greater Noida holds about a quarter of NCR's unsold homes. That is why this forecast is very conservative.
Investors are a primary buyer here, not a fringe. The workbook's flip test passes: at possession, 7 Peaks would sit about 14% above ATS Dolce-grade ready stock grown at 7% a year. Present it as an expectation with its date, never as a return.

Where 7 Peaks sits on price.

Below the launches' median

13% under the median of Sobha Aurum, Godrej Arden and Godrej Crown. The strongest price story in the workbook.

Above the old sector stock

About 2.5× Omicron's 15-year-old resale. It's the only red-flag concern, and it's permanent. Answer it by comparing with new launches.

Rent offsets, never income

Yields at possession run ~1.8–2.2% gross and ~1.2% net; rent offsets only part of the EMI. Never quote a yield.

How buyers search.

Monthly Google searches

India · seodata.dev estimates · — means too few to register

Buyers search by size and city. "3 BHK flats in Greater Noida" and its variants draw about 1,000 searches a month each, at under a dollar a click.
Eldeco's name is searched for its past projects. Green Meadows alone draws ~12,100 searches a month. 7 Peaks has to borrow that trust, one owner at a time.
No new project name registers. Not 7 Peaks, not Godrej Arden, not Sobha Aurum. Brand search is open ground for whoever builds it first.

Why the money goes where it goes.

Partners are the cheapest site visit

Channel-partner drives and investor evenings take 35% of the budget. ANAROCK finds the top 5% of partners bring 40–50% of bookings, so the money follows the few who close.

Digital stays targeted

Meta in NCR-East and Western UP, plus Google on "3 BHK Greater Noida" and the brand. No broad "luxury" audiences.

The Gulf waits for January

The workbook holds NRIs to referrals until Sustenance. ₹1.25 L tests the UAE in January–March, then scales on cost per visit.

Budget rule set by the promoter: marketing spend of 0.6–0.95% of each home's value. Average home ₹2.65 Cr, weighted 3 and 4 BHK at ₹13,000 BSP. Budget excludes channel-partner brokerage and Fixit's fee, and is reallocated weekly inside the approved total on cost per site visit. For context, ANAROCK says major brands keep total acquisition cost, brokerage included, at 2.5–2.8% of sales (May 2026).

Very conservative means cost per lead above the NCR range, site-visit and booking rates below the bottom of the market ranges, and only 65% of a quarter's bookings landing in that quarter. It also allows for a slower market: Noida and Greater Noida sales fell 20% in Apr–Jun 2026, Godrej's NCR projects outside Gurugram booked at most ~₹290 Cr between them that quarter, and site-visit no-shows run 30–45%. These are bookings sourced by this plan; channel-partner sales outside it come on top. The budget is sized so even this case stays inside 0.6–0.95% of the homes it sells.
